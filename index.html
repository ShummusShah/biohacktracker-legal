FAQ

Good to know

Is Biohack a substitute for medical advice?

No. Biohack is a personal tracking tool only — it does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. Always consult a qualified healthcare professional before starting, changing, or stopping any compound or dosing regimen.

What platforms does Biohack support?

Biohack is built for iOS and distributed through the App Store.

How does the 7-day free trial work?

New Premium subscribers get 7 days free. You won't be charged until the trial ends, and you can cancel anytime before then in your Apple ID subscription settings with nothing owed.

Is my data private?

Yes. Your dose, weight, and photo data is stored securely and never sold or shared with advertisers. Photos are kept in a private, access-controlled area only your account can reach. See our Privacy Policy for the full details.

Can I track custom compounds?

Yes. Alongside the built-in compound library, you can add your own custom compounds with their own half-life data.

Can I cancel my subscription anytime?

Yes. Premium is billed and managed entirely through the App Store, so you can cancel anytime in your Apple ID account settings — no need to contact us.
